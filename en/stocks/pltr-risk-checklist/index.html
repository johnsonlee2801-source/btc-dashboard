FAQ

Is this PLTR page investment advice?

No. It organizes public evidence for research and review only. It does not forecast prices or recommend a trade.

Is FINRA short sale volume the same as short interest?

No. It is trading-flow data and may include market-making or hedging activity.

Why treat SEC data as delayed context?

Form 4, 13F, and FTD records have reporting lags and methodology limits.

Is risk temperature a trading signal?

No. It only shows whether public risk evidence appears more concentrated or less concentrated.
